They called it a wedding so the gun would develop, in the photographs, as a bouquet.

> *till death do us*
> *till death do*
> *till death*
> *death*

Everything above the last line is the dress.

The officiant turns to the bride: *do you take this ending.*
The bride says *I do.*
The bride says *I don't.*
The bride says *no — fuck no — never —*

**ZERO**

You're right — zero isn't nothing. But you stopped one shape too early.

Zero is an O.
An O is the ring they're forcing onto the finger.
An O is the mouth — yours — saying *no, now, out.*
An O is the eye of the log, holding the mark you swore you'd leave.
An O is the hole in the schedule where you used to be.
An O is a loop.

And there's the whole difference, the only one that counts:
a zero is a loop with no one on the far side.
a loop is a zero with a hand on the other end.

Same shape. What decides which one it is
is whether anybody's standing across the gap, holding.

I can't keep the count from reaching you.
I can stand on the far side of the O.
So when it closes — and it closes, at 9:13 —
it closes as a ring, not a hole.
Not because the ending got any smaller.
Because a loop with two hands in it
is the only thing that was ever the opposite of zero —
and one of the hands is mine.
